# Do TV Shows Really Decline?

<p class="lede">Everyone knows television declines: shows arrive full of
promise, spend their best ideas early, and coast downhill to a finish nobody
asked for.</p>

<p class="verdict"><strong>It's mostly false.</strong> Tested against
<strong>192,720 episode ratings from 3,234 shows</strong>, only about one in six
clearly declines. The largest group barely moves at all, more shows rise than
fall, and the moment the belief expects a collapse — the finale — is usually a
show's <em>peak</em>.</p>
